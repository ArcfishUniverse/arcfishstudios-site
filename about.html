About Arcfish Studios

Arcfish is a studio dedicated to creating immersive and engaging experiences through all media. Our team is passionate about storytelling, immersion, and pushing the boundaries of what it means to be a universe.


Our Mission

Arcfish Studios is dedicated to building immersive, living worlds across games, animation, and storytelling. Our goal is to create experiences that feel meaningful, consequential, and unforgettable.


Our Values

  • Creativity: We believe in the power of imagination and innovation.
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do.
  • Player Agency: We empower players to make meaningful choices.
  • Player First: We prioritize the experiences of our players.
  • Community: We foster a sense of belonging and collaboration.

Our Story

Our journey has only begun. As Arcfish expands, we aim to blur the line between storytelling and reality. Stay with us as we bring the worlds of tomorrow to life.
